# Vareto

> Vareto is an AI-native financial planning and analysis (FP&A) platform for building financial models, budgets, and forecasts — headcount planning, sales revenue forecasting, P&L / balance sheet / cash flow modeling, and board reporting — with real-time multiplayer collaboration and data integrations across ERP, HRIS, CRM, data warehouse, and spreadsheet tools.
